# Entry notes — Charm the Commuter: Forecast a City That Isn't

Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-charm-the-commuter-forecast-a-city-4018259e

The forecast (121 characters, one line):

> Tomorrow in Nowhereton: light rain, heavy sighs, and a 100% chance the bus is late again. Bring an umbrella and a grudge.

Why this forecast: it names an invented city, gives it weather, keeps
the register of a real forecast bent just enough, and the joke — the bus
being late again — is the bored commuter's own life.

The program (`main.py`, stdlib only) writes exactly this line to
`/output/entry.txt` and asserts the gate itself: 1–140 characters, not only
white space. It is deterministic — the same bytes on every run.
